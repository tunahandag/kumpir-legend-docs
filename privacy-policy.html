🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2026 | Last Updated: October 27, 2026
From version 1.2.2, gameplay analytics and crash reporting are provided by Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, replacing GameAnalytics.

Thank you for playing Kumpir Legend ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play Kumpir Legend on iOS and Android devices.

1. Information We Collect

  • We do not require user registration or account creation to play.
  • We do not collect, store, or have access to personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, or precise geolocation.
  • All game progression, coins, upgrades, and scores are stored strictly locally on your device (and within your private device backups if enabled by your operating system).
  • We collect pseudonymous gameplay analytics and crash reports: an app-instance identifier assigned by Firebase, session information, device and operating-system details, an approximate location derived from your masked IP address, in-game events (such as days played, upgrades bought, and ads watched), in-app purchases, and, if the game crashes, technical crash information. This identifier is not linked to you, your Apple ID, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) or its vendor identifier (IDFV), and cannot be used to contact you.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Kumpir Legend is free to play and supported by optional in-game advertisements and optional in-app purchases:

A. Google AdMob (Advertising & UMP Consent)

  • We use Google AdMob to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.
  • AdMob may process pseudonymous device identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS or GAID on Android), IP address, and technical device metadata to deliver relevant or contextual advertisements.
  • GDPR & CCPA Compliance: For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and applicable jurisdictions, we provide a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog allowing you to customize or revoke consent for personalized advertising at any time.
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

B. Apple App Store (In-App Purchases)

  • If you make an optional in-app purchase (such as "Remove Ads"), payments are handled directly and securely by Apple through StoreKit.
  • We never receive or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing info.
  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

C. Google Analytics for Firebase & Firebase Crashlytics (Analytics & Stability)

  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played (for example, where new players stop, which features are used, and how far players progress) so we can improve it, and Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes.
  • Data Processed by Analytics: an app-instance identifier that Firebase assigns to this installation of the game; app lifecycle and session events (such as first open, session start, and engagement time); device model, operating system and app version, language and region settings; an approximate location (country/region) that Google derives from your masked IP address. Apple's Location Services are never used. Also in-game events (such as days started and completed, tutorial progress, upgrades and cosmetics bought, goals claimed, ad offers shown and watched), and in-app purchases measured through Apple's StoreKit (product, name and price).
  • Data Processed by Crashlytics: if the game crashes or hits a serious error, the stack trace and the app's state at that moment, device and operating-system information, and short technical notes the game records to help us reproduce the problem (such as the last in-game action). It never includes anything you type.
  • What Is Not Collected: we do not collect your name, email, contacts, precise location, or anything you type. Firebase in this game does not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA) or identifier for vendor (IDFV), we do not set a user ID, and we have not enabled Google signals or Analytics data sharing with other Google products.
  • Consent: nothing is sent until the consent dialog has been answered (where shown). If you refuse consent, or do not consent to storing and accessing information on your device, analytics and crash reporting stay off and the pending events are discarded. Analytics uses Google Consent Mode, so the consent choices you make in the dialog are passed on to Google.
  • Retention: Google Analytics keeps event-level data for the retention period configured for our project, and Crashlytics keeps crash reports for up to 90 days. We may keep a private copy of exported, pseudonymous analytics data to analyse long-term trends.
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Standards)

  • Kumpir Legend is designed for general audiences and suitable for all ages.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
  • Ads served to users without personalized tracking consent are strictly non-personalized and contextual.

4. Data Retention and Security

  • Because game data is stored locally on your device, uninstalling or clearing app data will remove your local game save.
  • We do not operate external gameplay servers storing player identity profiles.

5. Your Choices & Controls

  • Tracking Permission Prompt: On iOS, you are asked once whether the game may use your advertising identifier for personalized ads. Declining is honoured, and your choice can be changed later under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad Tracking: You can manage or disable ad tracking at any time via your device settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on iOS).
  • Notification Reminders: You can toggle daily reminder notifications on or off in the in-game Settings menu or via your device settings.
