🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 24, 2026 | Last Updated: August 24, 2026

Thank you for playing Kumpir Legend ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play Kumpir Legend on iOS and Android devices.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

  • We do not require user registration or account creation to play.
  • We do not collect, store, or have access to personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, or precise geolocation.
  • All game progression, coins, upgrades, and scores are stored strictly locally on your device (and within your private device backups if enabled by your operating system).

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Kumpir Legend is free to play and supported by optional in-game advertisements and optional in-app purchases:

A. Google AdMob (Advertising & UMP Consent)

  • We use Google AdMob to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.
  • AdMob may process pseudonymous device identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS or GAID on Android), IP address, and technical device metadata to deliver relevant or contextual advertisements.
  • GDPR & CCPA Compliance: For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and applicable jurisdictions, we provide a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog allowing you to customize or revoke consent for personalized advertising at any time.
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

B. Apple App Store (In-App Purchases)

  • If you make an optional in-app purchase (such as "Remove Ads"), payments are handled directly and securely by Apple through StoreKit.
  • We never receive or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing info.
  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Standards)

  • Kumpir Legend is designed for general audiences and suitable for all ages.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
  • Ads served to users without personalized tracking consent are strictly non-personalized and contextual.

4. Data Retention and Security

  • Because game data is stored locally on your device, uninstalling or clearing app data will remove your local game save.
  • We do not operate external gameplay servers storing player identity profiles.

5. Your Choices & Controls

  • Ad Tracking: You can manage or disable ad tracking at any time via your device settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on iOS).
  • Notification Reminders: You can toggle daily reminder notifications on or off in the in-game Settings menu or via your device settings.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: support@kumpirlegend.com
  • Support Portal: https://tunahandag.github.io/kumpir-legend-docs/support.html
