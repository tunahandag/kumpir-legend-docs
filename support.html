❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I restore my purchases (e.g., "Remove Ads")?

Open the in-game Settings menu (accessible from the pause screen or main menu) and tap "Restore Purchases" (Satın Alımları Geri Yükle). Ensure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Where is my game progress saved?

Your game save data is stored locally on your device. If you backup your device using iCloud or iTunes, your game save is automatically included.

How do I manage or turn off notifications?

You can toggle notifications inside the game via Settings → Notifications, or in your device settings under Settings → Notifications → Kumpir Legend.

The game is lagging or crashing. What should I do?

Ensure your device is updated to the latest iOS version, and close unused background apps. If the issue persists, please send your device model and iOS version to our support email.
